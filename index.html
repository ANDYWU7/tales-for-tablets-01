How to use the storybook

Swipe left or right across a page, or use the arrows to turn it. The marks between the arrows show your progress; a new chapter becomes available after you complete the activity on the current chapter.

Drag supplies into the crate, fit your drawn parts into their outlines in any order, and drag the rocket to its landing spot. Each movable object must be dropped at its destination. Both fins use your single fin drawing.

Choose Read to me to hear the story. With a keyboard, use Tab to select a movable object and Space or Enter to pick it up. Move it with the arrow keys, then press Space or Enter to drop it. Hold Shift to move farther, or press Escape to cancel. When you are not moving an object, the arrow keys turn pages.

# Sound credits

- `dog-soft-bark.mp3`: “Smal_Dog_Barking.wav” by ivolipa, https://freesound.org/people/ivolipa/sounds/332457/. Creative Commons CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Bundled Freesound high-quality MP3 preview. The app decodes the recording, skips leading silence, and plays a short excerpt at a normalized, gentle level with softened attack/release and a low-pass filter.
- `assemble-soft.ogg`: `impactWood_light_000.ogg` from Kenney’s Impact Sounds pack, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds. Creative Commons CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. File is unmodified; playback is quieter, filtered, and faded in/out.
- Rocket exhaust, paper rustling, tape tearing, metallic funnel sounds, landing, and interface sounds are original procedural effects implemented in `story-audio.js` using the browser’s native Web Audio API.
